Project 05

Real Estate Client Onboarding System

An automated client onboarding workflow for real estate teams — integrating Google Drive, Forms, Sheets, and Gmail into a seamless new-client pipeline that eliminates manual handoffs.

Google Workspace Google Forms Gmail Real Estate

The Problem

Real estate teams onboard new clients through a maze of emails, phone calls, and scattered documents. Intake forms get lost, follow-up emails are forgotten, documents end up in the wrong folders, and agents waste hours on administrative tasks instead of selling. Every new client means repeating the same manual steps — and every missed step risks losing the deal.

The Solution

I designed and built a fully automated onboarding system using only free Google Workspace tools. A single form submission triggers the entire pipeline — creating client folders, sending welcome emails, populating tracking sheets, and notifying the assigned agent. No manual steps, no missed follow-ups.

For Agents

Instant Slack/email notification when a new client submits their intake form. Client folder pre-built with templates. Tracking dashboard auto-updated.

For Clients

Professional welcome email with next steps. All documents organized in one place. Clear timeline and expectations from day one.

Process & Workflow

1

Client Intake Form

Custom Google Form captures all essential client information — contact details, property preferences, budget range, timeline, and special requirements.

2

Automatic Folder Creation

Google AppScript creates a structured client folder in Drive with pre-loaded templates for contracts, disclosures, and correspondence.

3

Welcome Email Dispatch

Gmail sends a personalized welcome email with next steps, team contact info, and links to their client folder and relevant documents.

4

Dashboard Update

Client details auto-populate the master tracking sheet — pipeline stage, assigned agent, intake date, and all form responses.

5

Agent Notification

The assigned agent receives an instant notification with the client summary and a direct link to their folder and tracking entry.

Key Features

  • 01
    Zero manual steps — from form submission to agent notification, everything runs automatically via AppScript triggers.
  • 02
    Structured folder hierarchy — consistent naming conventions and pre-loaded templates ensure every client gets the same professional experience.
  • 03
    Real-time dashboard — master tracking sheet gives the team instant visibility into pipeline status and workload distribution.
  • 04
    Free tools only — the entire system runs on Google Workspace with no paid subscriptions or third-party dependencies.
